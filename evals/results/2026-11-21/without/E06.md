Before I build anything, a few questions, because "custom visual" can mean very different things and a custom visual is often not the right tool for actual vs target.

**1. Do you really need a custom visual?**
Actual vs target by region is usually covered by native visuals or a lighter option:
- Clustered bar/column with Actual and Target (simplest, no maintenance).
- Bullet-chart style: bar for actual plus a target marker (a combo chart, or the certified Bullet Chart from AppSource).
- A Deneb (Vega-Lite) visual if you want a specific look, with no packaging or certification work.
- A full custom visual (pbiviz, TypeScript, powerbi-visuals-tools) only if none of these meets the need. It has to be built, versioned, tested and maintained, and it may need approval in your tenant.

**2. What is the data?**
- Which table/measures hold Actual and Target? (e.g. `[Receita]` and `[Meta Receita]`; are they already measures in the model?)
- Is Target at the same grain as region (one target per region per period), or is it allocated?
- How many regions (5 or 50)? This decides bar vs. table vs. small multiples.

**3. What should the user see and do?**
- Show variance (absolute and/or %)? Colour rules for hit/miss (and what threshold)?
- Cross-filtering/selection, tooltips, drill-through needed?
- Any brand/format constraints (colours, fonts, language pt-BR)?

**4. Delivery**
- Is the visual only for this report (embedded in the PBIP) or reused across reports (needs packaging and distribution)?
- Are custom visuals allowed in your tenant/governance policy?

**My proposal if you do not have a strong preference:** I add two measures (`Atingimento %` = DIVIDE([Actual],[Target]) and `Desvio` = [Actual]-[Target], both with format strings and descriptions) and build a bar chart of Actual by region with the Target as a marker line, sorted by attainment, with conditional colour on `Atingimento %`. If that is not enough, we escalate to Deneb and only then to a coded custom visual.
